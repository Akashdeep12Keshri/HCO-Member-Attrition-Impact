HCO Member Attrition Cost Impact Prediction

Business Objective:
For a Health Care Organization (HCO), losing a member doesn’t just mean churn — it means revenue loss.

Healthcare organizations want to predict annual healthcare cost per member to:

* Identify high-cost members
* Iprove budgeting & actuarial planning
* Support proactive care management



Goal:
Predict the expected annual cost / revenue impact if a member is at risk of leaving.
